Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to Free Image Compressor. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and image compression tools.

1. Images You Process

Our image compression tools are designed to process images directly in your web browser.

Images selected for compression are processed locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to our server for image compression.

Because image processing happens in your browser, your original image remains on your device unless you independently choose to share or upload it somewhere else.

Free Image Compressor provides browser-based tools for compressing JPG, PNG and WebP images.
