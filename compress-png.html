What Is a PNG Compressor?

A PNG compressor is a tool designed to reduce the size of PNG image files. Smaller files can be easier to store, share and use on websites.

PNG is commonly used for graphics, screenshots, logos, illustrations and images that need transparency.

How to Compress a PNG Image

  1. Choose a PNG image from your device.
  2. Select your preferred compression quality.
  3. Click the Compress PNG button.
  4. Compare the original and processed file sizes.
  5. Download your PNG image.

Why Reduce PNG File Size?

Large PNG files can consume more storage space and may take longer to transfer or load online.

Reducing image file size can be useful when preparing images for websites, documents, email attachments and online forms.

Is My PNG Image Uploaded?

No. The image processing happens directly in your browser. Your PNG image does not need to be uploaded to a remote server.

This means you can process an image while keeping the original file on your own device.
